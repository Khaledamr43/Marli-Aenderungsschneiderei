# Step 4 — Information Architecture & Content Plan
_Marli Änderungsschneiderei & Second-Hand · one-page portfolio site_

## Content matrix (per page section)

| # | Section | Photos needed | Copy needed | Notes / SEO |
|---|---------|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Hero | 1 hero image: shop front or atelier detail, landscape, high-res | Shop name + identity line: "Änderungsschneiderei & Second-Hand in Kolbermoor" | H1 carries the main local keyword; must load fast (LCP image) |
| 2 | About Marli | 1–2: owner portrait, at work at the machine | 80–120 words: who, since when (opened 04/2026), background, what she loves about the craft | Personal trust anchor; name "Marli" in heading |
| 3 | What we do | 4–6 service shots: hemming, zipper repair, Dirndl/Tracht work, custom piece | 4–6 services, task-based names ("Hosen kürzen", "Reißverschluss tauschen", "Dirndl anpassen") + one line each | Task-based names match real search queries |
| 4 | Work gallery | 12–20 finished pieces + 4–6 before/after pairs, consistent background/light | Short captions per photo: garment + what was done | The heart of the page. Every image gets descriptive German alt text |
| 5 | Secondhand corner | 6–10: racks, curated pieces, shop interior atmosphere | 2–3 sentences: what kind of stock, changing weekly, "come browse" | No product data — atmosphere over catalog; "Second Hand Kolbermoor" keyword |
| 6 | Find us | 1 storefront photo (optional) | Address, opening hours, phone — exactly matching the Google Business listing (NAP consistency) | Google Maps embed + LocalBusiness structured data |
| 7 | Footer | — | Impressum + Datenschutzerklärung (standard German legal templates), Instagram link if available | Separate minimal legal URLs |

**Estimated photo total: 25–40 shots** → one photo session at the shop covers everything (plan in Step 10).

## To collect from Marli (stakeholder checklist)
1. Logo or preferred lettering of the shop name (any existing branding?)
2. Owner photo permission + short bio facts
3. 5–10 examples of past work she is proud of (for the gallery and before/after)
4. Exact opening hours + phone number as on Google
5. Instagram account — exists or not?
6. Any existing photos usable until the shoot happens

## Open questions (decide before design)
- **Tone of voice:** formal "Sie" vs. friendly "Du" — recommendation: warm "Sie" (older local clientele, Bavarian small town), decide with Marli
- **Shop name lettering:** typographic logo from the site's type system vs. hand-drawn feel
- **Before/after format:** side-by-side pairs vs. interactive slider (decide at wireframe stage)

## Content-first rule
No high-fidelity design until real photos exist — layouts built on stock photos always break with real content. Wireframes (Step 5) can proceed with greyboxes sized to the shot list above.
