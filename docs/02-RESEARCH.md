# Step 2 — Research
_Marli Änderungsschneiderei & Second-Hand, Kolbermoor · UX/UI case study_
_Structured for transfer to FigJam (each ## = one board section)._

## 1. Direct local competitor

### Mauritz Änderungsschneiderei, Kolbermoor — mauritz-aenderungsschneiderei.de
- One-page Squarespace site: Home / Über mich / Leistungen / Designs / Kontakt.
- Services as 5 icon cards: Anpassungen, Änderungen, Reparaturen, Maßanfertigungen, Express.
- Strong personal branding: photo + story of the tailor ("20 Jahre Erfahrung, aus Leidenschaft").
- Gallery of own dress/Dirndl creations (plain image grid, no before/after).
- Repeated "Termin vereinbaren" CTA, but it only scrolls to a contact section — no real booking.
- Weaknesses: no prices, no opening hours visible, no secondhand, generic stock hero image, template feel.
- **Gap for Marli: no local competitor combines alterations + secondhand; none has hours/prices/booking done well.**

Other local players (directory listings only, mostly no real website): Anita Weiß (Kolbermoor), Tüler Ekrem (Kolbermoor), Schneiderei Irina Argutin (Bad Aibling), several in Rosenheim. Low digital competition locally.

## 2. Inspiration: modern alteration shops

### Die Änderei, Bayreuth — die-aenderei-bayreuth.de
- Best-in-class local tailor site. Address, hours, parking, WhatsApp, phone, email all above the fold.
- Informal "Du" tone, witty microcopy ("Faden verloren? Schreib uns!", "Wer nichts ändert, ist keine Änderungsschneiderei").
- 7 service cards incl. niche offers (Kunststopfen, Corporate Fashion, Theaterkostüme).
- Clear walk-in policy: no appointment needed, except bridal/evening wear.
- Contact form with file upload (photo of garment) + GDPR consent; embedded Google Maps.
- Heritage story (since 1945) builds trust.
- Takeaway: practical info first, personality in copy, low-friction contact.

### Change Into, München — changeinto.de
- Online alterations service: numbered 5-step "So funktioniert's" flow, e-commerce checkout, price list page.
- Sustainability positioning (upcycling, DHL GoGreen, recycled packaging).
- Founder story (25 years couture experience) as trust anchor.
- Per-city SEO landing pages (München, Berlin, Hamburg…) — good local SEO tactic.
- Takeaway: step-by-step process explanation and a public price list reduce inquiry friction. Full e-commerce is overkill for Marli.

## 3. Inspiration: secondhand boutiques

### Kleiderpracht, München — kleiderpracht.com
- Designer secondhand boutique, one-pager: Boutique / About / Labels / Annahme / Press / Contact.
- **No online shop** — Instagram feed IS the live catalog (pieces posted with price, sold via DM). Very low-maintenance model, ideal for small shops.
- Clear "Annahme/Kommission" page: what/how/when/where rules for people bringing clothes in.
- Owner personality + fashion quotes give the brand charm; press section builds credibility.
- Takeaway for Marli: secondhand section can be "how it works" + Instagram embed instead of real e-commerce.

### Vintage Berlin / Second Vintage (scanned briefly)
- Full webshops with category navigation; viable only with constant inventory photography — heavy maintenance. Confirms: skip e-commerce at launch.

## 4. Search intent (German market)

High-intent local queries (what people actually type):
- "Änderungsschneiderei in der Nähe" / "Schneiderei Kolbermoor" / "Schneiderei Rosenheim"
- Task-based: "Hose kürzen", "Kleid kürzen", "Rock kürzen", "Hose enger nähen", "Reißverschluss reparieren"
- High-value: "Brautkleid ändern/kürzen", "Dirndl ändern" (regional!), "Anzug ändern"
- Secondhand: "Second Hand Kolbermoor/Rosenheim", "Second Hand Kleidung in der Nähe"
- Price intent: "Hose kürzen Kosten/Preis" — a price list page captures this.

Implications:
- Name each service by the task customers search for (not trade jargon).
- Local SEO is the main channel: Google Business Profile + city names in page titles/copy.
- 100k+ monthly searches nationwide for Schneiderei-related terms; reviews strongly influence choice.

## 5. Key takeaways → design principles for Marli

1. **Unique positioning:** only shop in the area combining alterations + secondhand — make this dual identity the brand story.
2. **Essentials above the fold:** address, hours, phone/WhatsApp (Die Änderei pattern). Marli's Google listing exists; site must match it.
3. **Personal trust:** owner photo + story outperforms stock imagery (Mauritz, Change Into, Kleiderpracht all do this).
4. **Task-based service names** with a simple price list ("Hose kürzen ab X €").
5. **Secondhand without e-commerce:** "how it works" + Instagram-as-catalog (Kleiderpracht model).
6. **Low-friction contact:** WhatsApp + form with photo upload; state walk-in vs. appointment policy.
7. **Regional angle:** Dirndl/Tracht alterations are a Bavarian differentiator worth a dedicated mention.
8. **SEO baseline:** one page per core intent, local keywords, Google Maps embed, review prompts.
