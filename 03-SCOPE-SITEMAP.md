# Step 3 — Scope & Page Structure
_Marli Änderungsschneiderei & Second-Hand · UX/UI case study_

## Scope decision (stakeholder input, 15.07.2026)
The website is a **portfolio site**: it shows the shop's work, the products they sell, and where to find them. It is **one page**, with **no e-commerce** and **no call-to-action funnels** (no booking flows, no forms). Photos of past work and products carry the site.

## In scope
- Single scrolling page, fully responsive (mobile first — local "near me" searches are mostly mobile)
- Photo-heavy galleries: finished work, before/after pairs, secondhand pieces
- Owner/shop story section (personal trust — validated by research)
- Location block: address, opening hours, phone, embedded Google Map (passive info, not a CTA)
- Image optimization: lazy loading, responsive sizes, fast load on mobile data
- Local SEO basics: meta tags, structured data (LocalBusiness) matching the Google Business listing, alt text
- Legal pages required in Germany: Impressum & Datenschutzerklärung (small separate URLs linked from the footer)

## Out of scope
- E-commerce (cart, checkout, payments)
- Appointment booking, contact forms, newsletter
- CTAs / conversion funnels
- CMS or admin backend (content updates happen in code — fits the portfolio-piece goal)
- Blog, multi-language, customer accounts, multi-page architecture

## One-page structure (top to bottom)
1. **Hero** — shop name, one-line identity ("Änderungsschneiderei & Second-Hand in Kolbermoor"), large atmospheric photo
2. **About Marli** — owner photo + short story; the trust anchor
3. **What we do** — services shown visually: alterations, repairs, Dirndl/Tracht work, custom pieces
4. **Work gallery** — the heart of the page: lookbook-style grid, before/after pairs
5. **Secondhand corner** — photos of typical pieces; "come browse in the shop" framing (no shop system)
6. **Find us** — address, hours, phone, Google Maps embed
7. **Footer** — Impressum · Datenschutz · Instagram link (if available)

## Consequences for later steps
- Step 4 (IA & content plan): map photos/copy per section above; photo inventory is the critical path
- Step 5 (wireframes): one long-scroll wireframe (mobile + desktop)
- Step 9 (development): drop forms/CMS/booking from the original plan; static site is sufficient
- Step 10 (content): photo shoot is the main effort
